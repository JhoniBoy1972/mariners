We are always happy to assist our guests. Here are answers to some of the most common questions about dining at Mariners' Restaurant.

You can make a reservation through our Reservation page on the website or contact us directly. We recommend booking in advance, especially for weekends and special occasions.

Mariners' Restaurant is located at Upper Lake Road, Nuwara Eliya, Sri Lanka. We are situated in the beautiful Nuwara Eliya area and are easily accessible from the town.

We are open from 7:30 AM to 9:00 PM from Tuesday to Sunday. The restaurant is closed on Mondays.

Please let our team know about any dietary requirements or food allergies when making your reservation or before ordering. We will do our best to assist you with suitable menu options.
