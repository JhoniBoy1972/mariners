About Mariners' Restaurant

Good Food, Warm Hospitality, and Beautiful Moments in the Heart of Nuwara Eliya.

Mariners' Restaurant is a welcoming place to enjoy good food and great company in the beautiful surroundings of Nuwara Eliya. Our menu brings together popular Sri Lankan dishes and a variety of delicious meals prepared with care, making every visit a relaxed and enjoyable dining experience.

From flavorful rice and curry and sizzling kottu to biryani, noodles, short eats, refreshing drinks, and delicious desserts, our menu offers something for every taste. Whether you're visiting with family, meeting friends, or simply looking for a satisfying meal, Mariners' Restaurant is ready to welcome you with great food and friendly service.
