1. Reservations and Punctuality

We recommend making reservations in advance, especially during weekends, holidays, and busy periods. Guests are kindly requested to arrive on time for their reservation. Late arrivals may result in reduced dining time or the release of the table depending on availability.

2. Group Bookings

For large groups or special occasions, advance reservations may be required. Additional arrangements or conditions may apply depending on the size of the group and the nature of the booking.

3. Dietary Restrictions and Allergies

Please inform our staff about any food allergies, intolerances, or special dietary requirements before placing your order. We take reasonable precautions when preparing food; however, our kitchen handles various ingredients and we cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment.

4. Service and Restaurant Charges

Any applicable service charges, taxes, or other restaurant charges will be communicated where applicable and included in the final bill. Prices may vary depending on the selected menu items and services.

5. Outside Food and Beverages

Outside food and beverages are generally not permitted within the restaurant. Guests wishing to bring a celebration cake or other special items should contact Mariners' Restaurant in advance for approval.

6. Right to Refuse Service

Mariners' Restaurant reserves the right to refuse service or request a guest to leave if their behavior is abusive, threatening, disruptive, or negatively affects other guests or our staff.

7. Dress Code

Guests are kindly requested to follow the restaurant's dress code and maintain a neat and presentable appearance.

Collar-less T-shirts, skinnies, sleeveless dresses, three quarters, shorts and slippers are not allowed.

Management reserves the right to refuse entry or service if the dress code is not followed.

8. Children and Guest Conduct

Children are welcome at Mariners' Restaurant. Parents and guardians are responsible for ensuring that children behave appropriately and do not disturb other guests or restaurant operations.

9. Lost Property

Guests are responsible for their personal belongings while visiting the restaurant. Mariners' Restaurant cannot accept responsibility for belongings that are lost, stolen, or left unattended on the premises. Any property found will be handed over to the restaurant management.

10. Pricing and Menu Changes

Menu items, availability, and prices are subject to change without prior notice due to seasonal availability, supplier changes, or other circumstances. We make every effort to ensure that the information displayed on our website and menus is accurate and up to date.
