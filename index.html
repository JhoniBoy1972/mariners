About Mariners' Restaurant

A warm dining experience in Nuwara Eliya, bringing together delicious food, Sri Lankan flavors, and beautiful moments by Gregory Lake.

Located on Upper Lake Road in Nuwara Eliya, Mariners' Restaurant offers a welcoming place to enjoy a variety of delicious dishes in a beautiful lakeside setting. Our menu brings together Sri Lankan favorites and other popular dishes, prepared with fresh ingredients and care.

From comforting rice and curry and flavorful kottu to biryani, noodles, short eats, fresh juices, desserts, and more, there is something for everyone to enjoy. Whether you're sharing a meal with family, meeting friends, or enjoying a special occasion, Mariners' Restaurant is a place to relax, eat well, and make lasting memories.

Meet Our Kitchen Team

Our kitchen team brings care, skill, and passion to every dish, preparing a variety of Sri Lankan favorites and delicious meals for our guests.

From traditional rice and curry to kottu, biryani, noodles, and more, every dish is prepared to deliver fresh flavors and a memorable dining experience.
